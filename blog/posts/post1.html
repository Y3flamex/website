# my first real 9-5 job

Published on: April 7, 2025

## working sucks

working sucks. thats the truth. you feel like youre slaving away for meanial pieces of paper. yet we all need to work, its not only our duty to be providers for the people that we love, but the devil wander in idle minds. staying busy is one of the most important things someone can do in order to improve their life. i have worked 2 jobs, both retail, both sucked. they were the easiest jobs in the world and yet they sucked, because jobs suck. color me suprised when i get a job that doesnt suck

## lockheed martin

yeah i work at lockheed martin, so what? we all need to make a paycheck somehow and i don't want to flip burgers all my life. i ended up getting my ipc certifications at valencia college and boom, they put me on with a job. this job has been a complete change. i cant go into detail due to the nature of the work but we work closely with the military (obviously lol.) working this job has been teaching me discipline, i have to wake up in the morning at the same time, go to work, do things that i don't wanna do for the sake of my future. like right now as im typing this im procrastinating doing my internal certs lol. this job is awesome though, my coworkers don't suck, my boss is the second chillest guy ive ever met, and its piss easy. im just not staying stagnant though, as easy as that is in my position. my cybersecurity goals are more important than staying stagnant. lockheed will pay for my sec+ so thats absolutely a good thing im going to take advantage of. who knows, maybe lockheed would want me to work for them as a pentester.

## to a brighter future

i don't have much to say for my conclusion, all i know is that im feeling good about this job. i hope that it lasts. im really happy for once, and thats really hard for me to achieve most of the time. im never satisfied, but for once, im satisfied
